# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 

## Overview

This app allows users to: 12/4/2025
- Share YouTube videos.
- Download video thumbnails to the public folder (/Pictures).
- Copy video descriptions, titles, hashtags, keywords, and transcripts.
- Use Gemini AI to generate new scripts from video transcripts.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled.

## Information We Collect

This app does **not** collect or store any personal information. The app operates locally on your device and does not require authentication.

## Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions. It only saves thumbnails to the public folder (/Pictures) on your device.

## How We Use Your Information

Since no personal data is collected, there is no data usage or sharing. All operations are performed locally on your device.

## Data Security

We prioritize your data security:
- No data is transmitted to external servers.
- All processing is done locally on your device.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party services for data collection or processing.

## Your Rights

As no personal data is collected, there are no rights to access, modify, or delete data. You retain full control over your device and files.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in app functionality. Updates will be reflected here.
